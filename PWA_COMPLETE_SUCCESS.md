## 📱 **How Users Install SafariBus PWA**

### **Android (Chrome):**
1. Open http://localhost:5000
2. Tap menu (⋮) → "Add to Home screen"
3. Tap "Install" → App installed on home screen

### **iPhone (Safari):**
1. Open http://localhost:5000
2. Tap Share (📤) → "Add to Home Screen"
3. Tap "Add" → App installed on home screen
